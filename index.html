One team for the whole system

From the panels on your roof to the earth rod in the ground, we fit every part and show you how to monitor it.

  • Solar panel installation

    Roof or ground frames, wired neatly and angled to catch the Benin sun.

  • Hybrid inverter setup

    Inverters that switch between solar, battery, NEPA and generator on their own.

  • Lithium battery setup and replacement

    New lithium banks, or replacing tired batteries in a system you already have.

  • Earthing

    Proper earthing protects your appliances, your inverter and the people in the house.

  • Repairs and troubleshooting

    Emergency fixes for systems that stop charging, trip off or drain too fast.

  • CCTV installation

    Cameras for your home, shop or compound, powered by your solar system.
